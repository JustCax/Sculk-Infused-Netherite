# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue in Sculk-Infused Netherite, please **do not open a
public GitHub issue**.

Contact the maintainers privately instead:

- **JustCax** — Discord: `caxtmt`
- **BlackCatGirl_08** — Discord: `blackcatgirl_08`

Please include:

- the mod version and the Minecraft version you were using
- the loader version (NeoForge) and the versions of Advanced Netherite,
  Deeper and Darker and Accessories
- a description of the problem and what you expected to happen
- steps to reproduce it, ideally with a minimal save or log excerpt

## What to expect

- You will get an acknowledgement that the report was received.
- You will be told whether the report is accepted, and what the next step is.
- If a fix is needed, it ships in a normal release. We do not ask reporters to
  publish a proof of concept before a fix exists.

## Scope

In scope:

- crashes, corruption of saves, or item duplication caused by the mod
- server-side exploits that affect other players
- anything that lets a client gain items or progress it should not be able to

Out of scope:

- the behaviour of the Deep Dark, the Warden, or vanilla mechanics
- the balance and tuning of Infusion levels and abilities
- incompatibilities with other mods, unless the mod's own code is at fault
- missing features, which belong in the issue tracker
