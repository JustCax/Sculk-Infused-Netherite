Expand your progression into the Deep Dark with Sculk-Infused Netherite equipment, custom enchantments, powerful Warden-themed utilities, and the Sculk Workbench.

## About

Sculk-Infused Netherite is a late-game progression addon for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge.

Upgrade Advanced Netherite Diamond equipment using Deep Dark resources in the custom **Sculk Workbench**, then develop that equipment through a **15-level Infusion System** until it is fully stabilized and perfected.

The design goal is equipment that keeps progressing across a long playthrough, rather than acting as a one-time gear swap.

## The Infusion Update — version 1.1.0

Every Sculk-Infused weapon, tool and armor piece now has its own progression path from **Infusion Level 1 to 15**. Across the nine supported pieces this is **135 unique abilities**, and they are cumulative: a Level 15 item keeps everything a Level 1 item had and adds 14 more unlocks.

Progression is divided by three **Stabilization** milestones, performed at the Sculk Workbench:

| Level | Stabilization | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | First Stabilization | 250 Stored Infusion |
| 10 | Deep Stabilization | 600 Stored Infusion |
| 15 | Harmonic Stabilization | 1200 Stored Infusion |

A piece cannot level past a milestone until that Stabilization is done, so leveling and stabilizing alternate throughout a run.

**Stored Infusion** is carried in the reusable **Infusion Vessel**, which holds up to 3000. It is earned by doing Deep Dark things rather than by crafting: killing Deep Dark mobs, killing Deeper and Darker creatures, defeating Wardens, activating natural Sculk Shriekers, summoning a Warden, or absorbing a real Warden Sonic Boom with a Sonic Aegis.

## Features

### Progression

- 9 equipment progression paths — 5 tools and weapons, 4 armor pieces
- 15 Infusion Levels per piece, **135 cumulative abilities** in total
- Three Stabilizations at Levels 5, 10 and 15
- Reusable Infusion Vessel with 3000 Stored Infusion
- Progression is stored on the item itself — enchantments, durability and custom names survive every level
- Existing Sculk-Infused gear can be brought into the Infusion System, so older worlds stay valid

### Equipment

- Sculk-Infused Diamond Netherite sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel and hoe
- Sculk-Infused Diamond Netherite helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots
- Sculk Workbench crafting and Stabilization station

### Armor set system

A fully developed set gains a layer the individual pieces do not have. Every tier requires all four pieces at the listed level with the matching Stabilization.

- **Level 5 — Deep Link** — cross-piece interactions
- **Level 10 — Sculk Relay** — unlocks the Synchrony Meter, which builds from real armor events
- **Level 15 — Deep Synchrony** — at maximum Synchrony, manually activated, enhances all four armor subsystems

### Enchantments

Nine Deep Dark-themed enchantments. Resonant Fracture, Warden's Wrath, Deep Listener, Resonant Excavation, Deep Current, Seismic Thread, Pressure Sense, Earthquake and Echo Streak. Several of them scale directly with Infusion Level, so enchanting and leveling are complementary.

### Warden-themed utilities

- **Warden Ring** — ring slot accessory, +1 max health and Resistance I while worn
- **Sculk-Infused Golden Apple**
- **Sonic Aegis** and **Cracked Sonic Aegis**
- **Sonic Aegis Shard**
- **Echo Seal**
- **Quieting Sigil**
- **Vibration Decoy**
- **Infusion Vessel**

### World integration

- Ancient City loot integration
- Deeper and Darker Ancient Temple loot integration
- Deep Dark exploration is the intended main source of progression material

### Quality of life

- Custom advancement progression tree, including a **Perfected** advancement per piece
- JEI integration for Sculk Workbench recipes
- Enchanting Infuser compatibility
- Custom tooltips, particles and sound effects
- Multiplayer-compatible progression
