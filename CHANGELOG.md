All notable changes to Sculk-Infused Netherite are recorded here.
1.1.0 — The Infusion Update
The headline release. Sculk-Infused equipment stops being a one-time upgrade and
becomes a progression system.
Added
15 Infusion Levels for every Sculk-Infused weapon, tool and armor piece.
135 cumulative Infusion abilities across the nine supported pieces, 15 each.
Abilities stack: a Level 15 item keeps everything a Level 1 item had.
Stabilization at Infusion Level 5, 10 and 15 — First, Deep and Harmonic
Stabilization, performed at the Sculk Workbench. A piece cannot level past a
milestone until that Stabilization is completed.
Infusion Vessel, a reusable item that banks Stored Infusion and holds up to
3000. Stored Infusion is earned through Deep Dark activity, not crafted.
Deep Link, Sculk Relay and Deep Synchrony, a set progression layer for armor.
Deep Link at four pieces on Level 5, Sculk Relay plus the Synchrony Meter at
Level 10, Deep Synchrony at Level 15.
Four new enchantments: Deep Current, Seismic Thread, Pressure Sense and
Echo Streak, bringing the total to nine.
Advancements for completing the new progression, including a Perfected
advancement per equipment piece.
Sculk Workbench expansion and further Deep Dark integration.
Migration support so existing Sculk-Infused equipment can enter the Infusion
System.
Notes
Progression is stored on the item itself, so enchantments, durability and custom
names are preserved across every level and every Stabilization.
Progression systems are multiplayer-compatible and work in a client and server
environment.
