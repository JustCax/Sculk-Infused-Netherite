# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences and levels of experience.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy towards other community members.

Remember that this is a two-person volunteer project. Nobody is being paid, and
almost every interaction is someone choosing to spend their free time on this.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Dismissing or belittling a report, or telling someone their issue is not
  worth raising
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the issue tracker, pull
requests, discussions, and any other space where someone is representing the
project. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the
project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct,
report it to the maintainers privately. **Please do not open a public issue
for this.**

- **JustCax** — Discord: `caxtmt`
- **BlackCatGirl_08** — Discord: `blackcatgirl_08`

Include as much detail as you are comfortable with: what happened, where, when,
and whether anyone else saw it. If you are not sure whether something crosses
the line, report it anyway.

Reports are handled confidentially. We will respect your privacy and will not
publish details of the report without your agreement.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and
will take whatever action they judge appropriate and in the interest of a
safe, welcoming community. This can include a private correction, a public
warning, being banned from project spaces temporarily, or a permanent ban.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the other
maintainers.

## Scope of enforcement

A maintainer who receives a report is expected to keep it confidential and to
act fairly and consistently, taking into account the privacy and safety of
everyone involved.
